How the BeeHive upgraded figure is built

  • Supabase Pro $25/mo — 500 peak realtime connections, 8 GB database, backups, never pauses.
  • LiveKit Cloud Ship $50/mo — 1 000 concurrent connections, 150 000 connection-minutes, 250 GB downstream.
  • Render Starter $7/mo — always-on token backend (Free spins down and would add ~30 s to the first join).
  • A 300 × 2 h session is 36 000 connection-minutes: about four per month inside the Ship allowance, then ≈ R290 each. Fifty such sessions would still be under R15 000/mo.

Reading the comparison honestly

  • Vendors price per host; attendees are free everywhere. The strip assumes 10 hosts — at 9 hosts Teams Enterprise (R1 250) is still cheaper than BeeHive's flat R1 333; from 10 hosts up BeeHive is the cheapest option shown, and the gap widens with every host.
  • BeeHive's ceiling of 20 live mics and 25 cameras is a deliberate audience-mode design; Zoom, Teams and Meet allow more but degrade on the client just the same. Teams Enterprise's 1 000 is the largest interactive cap of the four.
  • Cloud recording and breakout rooms are the two features BeeHive does not have; neither is on the current roadmap.
  • The stub column is what runs right now — the upgrade is a plan change plus two environment variables (SUPABASE_PLAN, LIVEKIT_CLOUD_PLAN), not code.
